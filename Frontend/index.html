Our Mission & Vision

Ensuring the nation's food security by modernizing the livestock sector to build a Smart Bangladesh, and delivering government services quickly to grassroots farmers.

Transparency & Accountability

Keeping every step of vaccine and medicine allocation transparent through digital tracking, from the field level to the center.

Rapid Decision-Making

Controlling disease outbreaks and providing quick approval of demand requests through real-time reporting.

Boosting Production

Compiling accurate national-level production data for milk, meat, and eggs to inform future policy decisions.

Key Features Included in the Portal

Integrated workflows according to administrative level

Central & Regional Inventory

Real-time monitoring of vaccine, processed feed, and medicine stock at the national cold-chain store and district/upazila inventories.

Digital Demand & Approval

Upazila officers can create their specific demand requests and send them directly for approval to the district and central director.

Farm Production Tracking

A system for daily data entry and analysis of milk, eggs, and livestock breeding data from government and registered private farms.

ADP Budget & Project Tracking

Precise statistics on Annual Development Programme (ADP) budget allocation, field-level project progress, and expenditure.

Disease Outbreak Alert

Rapid central alert and formation of an emergency vaccine supply team whenever an infectious disease outbreak occurs in any upazila area.

Multi-Level Role Access

Separate role-based dashboards and control panels for upazila, district, central director, and farm management.
